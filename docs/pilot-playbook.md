# Pilot playbook

How to run the first pilots (plan M4): 3–5 companies use CompanyMgmt free for a month
with their real staff, and we learn what matters. Owner-run; this is the script.

## Who

20–100 person businesses in Bangladesh from the owner's network: agencies and offices
(people, attendance, leave, payroll, tasks) and one or two shops (till, dues, stock).
One decision-maker and one day-to-day contact per company.

## Before the setup call

- Make their workspace together or ask them to sign up; put them on the Growth plan
  trial (30 days) or set the plan by hand.
- Ask for a spreadsheet of staff (name, phone or email, department, joining date, leave
  left this year). The import template is in People → Import.
- Agree what they'll stop using during the pilot (a register, an Excel sheet, a
  WhatsApp group) so the product gets real use.

## Setup call (60 minutes)

1. Workspace details, branches (place them on the map if they want the location check),
   working day start and lateness grace.
2. Import people; fix the preview's problems together.
3. Leave types and holidays; payroll settings if they'll run payroll.
4. Invite managers by email; make staff accounts or share a join link / QR code
   (print the QR for the office wall).
5. For shops: products and prices, taxes (their accountant's numbers), register the
   till, cashiers set PINs.
6. Show Help (account menu → Help) and "Contact support".
7. Agree the success measures (below) and book the week-1 call.

## Week 1 check-in (20 minutes)

- Is everyone clocking in? Look at Reports together: attendance rate, late arrivals.
- Any staff who can't sign in? (Common: wrong workspace code; use the workspace's own
  address or a join link.)
- Collect the top three problems. Write each down with a decision (fix now / later / no).

## Week 4 review (45 minutes)

- What did it replace? What do they still do elsewhere, and why?
- Would they pay, which plan, and what's missing for that?
- Export their data with them (Settings → Data) so they know they can leave.
- Ask for a quote or case study if it went well (`docs/marketing/case-study.md`).

## Success measures (plan: "done when")

- At least three companies used it for two weeks with their real staff.
- Every pilot's top three problems are fixed or written down with a decision.

## What to watch (Settings → Platform, operators only)

Product use shows event counts across workspaces, never names or content: clock-ins,
leave requests, sales, tasks. A pilot with few events after week 1 needs a call.

## Feedback channel

"Contact support" in the app emails `SUPPORT_EMAIL` with the page and workspace; reply
from that inbox. Keep a simple sheet: company, date, problem, decision, status.
